# Women’s Labour Market Diagnostic in India

An interactive analytical dashboard examining spatial, demographic, and educational drivers of female labour force participation (LFPR), workforce engagement (WPR), and unemployment across Indian States using annual PLFS data (2017–2023).

## Analytical Structure

1. **National Diagnostic Overview:** Macro trajectories, headline gender gaps, and key structural patterns across 36 States/UTs.
2. **State Diagnostic Explorer & Context Profiles:** Detailed single-state diagnostic dossiers and pairwise state comparative analyses.
3. **Structural Fault Lines:** Spatial analysis of the female education U-curve, graduate unemployment, and rural-urban divergence.
4. **Employment & Enterprise Structure:** Compositional shares of persons engaged across non-agricultural enterprise types (NDAP Dataset 7131).
5. **Statistical Evidence, Predictive Limits & Methodology:** Formal hypothesis testing results (Phase 6) and 36-fold Leave-One-State-Out (LOSO) machine learning predictive evaluation (Phase 7).
6. **Forecasting Outlook:** 1-year time-series momentum forecasts ($t+1$) with empirical uncertainty intervals (80% nominal bounds) derived from expanding-window backtesting.

## Data Sources
- Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS), National Data & Analytics Platform (NDAP)
- Dataset 7129: Labour Market Indicators by Education, Gender, and Area (2017–2023)
- Dataset 7131: Persons Engaged in Industry by Enterprise Type (2017–2023)
